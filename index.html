For 4 bands: 2 digits, multiplier, tolerance. For 5 bands: 3 digits, multiplier, tolerance. The separated band is usually the tolerance band. Gold and silver often need manual correction.

01 / Frame

Hold the resistor straight across the guide. Fill most of the width and avoid shadows or glare.

02 / Scan

Capture a frame. The app samples a thin strip through the middle to propose band colors.

03 / Verify

Check each color and the direction. Tap bands in the photo or use the menus to fix a result.
